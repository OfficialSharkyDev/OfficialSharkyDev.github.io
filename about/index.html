About the studio

Sharkcrest Studios is an independent Roblox game development studio.

OUR APPROACH

Make room for play.

We want to build multiplayer games that give friends stories to tell afterward. That starts with a simple idea, clear rules, and enough room for players to surprise each other.

We founded Sharkcrest Studios on November 7, 2026. Our first game, HIDE YOUR MONSTER!, is where we’re putting that approach into practice.
