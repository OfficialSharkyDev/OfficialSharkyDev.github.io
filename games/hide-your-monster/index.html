THE GAMEPLAY

Ready for the knock?

01

Hide it fast

Carry your monster through the house and pick one of 80 hiding spots across four maps, from quick grabs to deep, risky hideouts.

02

Keep it calm

Monsters sneeze, rattle and glow at the worst moment. Soothe yours from a distance and stay out of the Inspector’s sight.

03

Outwit the Inspector

Trip the breaker, burst a pipe or set a decoy against Pickles, or play as the Inspector and hunt everyone else.

FOUR MAPS, FOUR MODES

Every round is different

MAPS

Four places to hide

Cozy House, Jolly Toy Factory, Grand Mansion and Mega Mart, each with its own hiding spots, routes and light switches.

MODES

Pick how you play

Players vs Pickles, Players vs Inspector, Lights Out and Monster Frenzy. The lobby votes on the mode and the map.

CLASSES

Choose your style

Hiders pick Caretaker, Dasher or Trickster. Inspectors pick Listener, Tracker or Warden. Each class has its own ability.

THE LOBBY

Welcome to Grumbleton Square

Something is hiding in the Big Wardrobe. Walk into a glowing doorway to peek inside any of the four maps, read the Tenant Rules, take a photo with Inspector Pickles, and climb the Sky Climb for a daily treasure while you wait for the next round.
